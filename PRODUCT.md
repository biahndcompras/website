# BIA Honduras — Product Context

**Status:** Approved for the current implementation  
**Owner:** BIA Honduras / BIA Foods  
**Surface:** Bilingual public website (`/` and `/careers`)

## Product purpose

Build trust and curiosity around BIA Honduras by telling a clear coffee-origin story, explaining the breadth of BIA Foods, and attracting future talent. The experience should feel enterprise-ready, warm, elegant, and rooted in Honduras without becoming a generic coffee catalogue.

## Audiences

- Honduran households and coffee consumers.
- Regional and international audiences looking for a credible BIA Foods story.
- Food-service, convenience, and commercial partners.
- Prospective employees and candidates exploring BIA as a place to build a career.

## Approved narrative

BIA Foods grew from the Grupo Mariposa ecosystem and operates through four strategic hubs: Coffee Hub, Culinary Hub, Snacks Hub, and Partners & Food Service. BIA Honduras is the Honduran pillar and a leader in the national coffee and food sector, integrating Café El Indio, Café Maya, Oro Puro, and Medalla.

The brand story connects Honduran coffee origin, local coffee farmers, quality, food safety, international standards, responsible value-chain practices, and a certified Great Place to Work culture.

## Language

The public experience is bilingual:

- Spanish is the default local language.
- English is fully supported.
- Language switching preserves route and scroll context.
- Careers content is available in both languages.

## Experience requirements

- Keep a full-screen video hero controlled by scroll, using the supplied local coffee film.
- Preserve the current editorial design profile: generous whitespace, precise typography, deep elegant blues, warm ivory, restrained copper, smooth transitions, and motion that adds depth rather than decoration.
- Use motion, Lenis, and scroll-linked effects without compromising readability or reduced-motion access.
- Include an editorial Home narrative and an independent Careers route.
- Do not invent statistics, certifications, locations, current vacancies, or other claims that require BIA approval.

## Content requiring BIA confirmation

- Official logo and brand-guideline files.
- Final approved copy for hubs, brands, origin regions, quality, and culture.
- Careers role families, hiring process, current openings, and contact details.
- Any final photography/video assets beyond the supplied local hero film.

## Verification target

Home and Careers must work in Spanish and English on desktop and mobile; the hero must scrub smoothly and degrade to a poster/fallback; anchors and route navigation must preserve language; and no legacy Cover or property-configurator content may remain in the active experience.
